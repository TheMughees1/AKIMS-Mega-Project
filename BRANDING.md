# AKIMS website branding — 5 November 2026

Source: https://akims.edu.pk/ (public homepage, fetched successfully).

## Applied identity

- Name: Akbar Institute of Medical Sciences, Bahawalpur. The homepage title/footer sometimes use singular “Science” and body copy sometimes uses “AIMS”; the portal retains AKIMS and the plural name from the main heading and logo.
- Website palette: dark emerald #022c22, primary #064e3b, accent #10b981, gold #d4af37. Website navigation also uses #166534. Gold is a decorative accent, not small text on white.
- Website typefaces: Inter and Playfair Display, bundled locally from Google Fonts with SIL Open Font Licenses.
- Exact original logo: https://akims.edu.pk/logo.png → assets/images/akims-logo.png. The supplied source is only 100 × 100 pixels; the interface uses it at small sizes without inventing a replacement.
- Building photograph: https://akims.edu.pk/img12%2011.jpeg → assets/images/akims-campus.jpeg.
- Campus session photograph: https://akims.edu.pk/img4.jpeg → assets/images/akims-campus-life.jpeg.

Original images are copied without pixel editing. Responsive CSS controls their displayed crop. Photographs and institutional identity remain attributable to the college; no open license for them is asserted. No third-party website scripts, analytics, embedded videos, or remote runtime fonts are copied.

## Public information

Help includes the homepage address, email, all three telephone numbers, website link and program names Pharm-D and B-Category. The homepage's additional accreditation/admission statements were not turned into portal policy or approval rules. Sample students, academic records, departmental assignments, payments and certificates remain fictional. Existing saved data is preserved; this is a branding change, not a migration to official academic records.

Logo appears in the role chooser, sidebar, favicon, document preview and printed documents. Downloaded HTML documents embed the logo so that their copy is self-contained. Building photos appear at entry and in student/alumni welcome panels; the campus session photograph appears in Help. The portal still opens from index.html and needs no installation.
